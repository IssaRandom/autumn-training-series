# Cut-out figure beside the text

| | |
|---|---|
| **Group** | 4. Pictures and video |
| **What it does for the story** | A person cut out of their photo (or drawn as a duotone illustration) stands beside or behind the text, so the page has a face without a full photo block. |
| **Seen on** | BBC Sport "The slow death of the screamer" (blue and red duotone drawings of players mid-shot beside the column, `shots/bbc-screamer-1280-013.jpg`) and BBC "Olympic battle royale" (cut-out athletes on gradients). One site (BBC, two articles). **Seen on 1.** |
| **Phone version** | BBC moves the figure above the text block, or behind the card at reduced contrast. |
| **How to build (vanilla)** | Transparent WebP/PNG cut-out, `position: sticky` in a side column on desktop; duotone with an SVG filter or `mix-blend-mode`. |
| **Cost / risks** | Low weight; cutting out photos takes time. |
| **Reduced motion** | Static. |
| **Use when** | Athlete stories, coach profiles. Sailing: a sailor hiking, cut out against the dark page. |
| **Don't use when** | The photo's background tells part of the story. |
