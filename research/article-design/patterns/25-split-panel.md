# Split panel (two sides at once)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Group** | 4. Pictures and video |
| **What it does for the story** | The screen is cut in two (here on a diagonal) to show two people or places at the same moment, like a phone call. |
| **Seen on** | BBC News "The boat that disappeared" (the man on the boat and the helpline volunteer on either side of a white diagonal, with the call transcript as short lines; `shots/bbc-boat-375-040.jpg`). **Seen on 1.** |
| **Phone version** | Kept on phones, stacked top and bottom with the diagonal cut. |
| **How to build (vanilla)** | Two images in one sticky figure, `clip-path: polygon(...)` on each; the dividing line can slide in with a scroll-driven animation. |
| **Cost / risks** | Low. Needs two matching images. |
| **Reduced motion** | Static split. |
| **Use when** | Coach and sailor, on the water vs on shore, two boats on different sides of the course. |
| **Don't use when** | The two sides aren't happening at the same time. |
