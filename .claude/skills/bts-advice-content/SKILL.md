# Advice Hub and venue guides

## Why (from James & Kai's Nov 2026 strategy chat)
- Website = main converter for the whole funnel. Give value first; coaching is a discreet "Coaching" tab/link, never the pitch (Alex Hormozi-style: give, give, give, links available).
- Top of funnel: talks/Q&A at Opti and Topper events (and early ILCA 4) — "what training exists" unbiased list (ILCA UK regional/national, academies, private groups, club open training) with BTS among them.
- Mid funnel: new-to-class ILCA sailors/parents — cold outreach offering the guide, not selling coaching ("when I moved up, we were lost…").
- Venue guides give a reason to message anyone entering an event at that venue.
- Shareable web link beats a PDF; aim to get ILCA UK to share it.
- Fitness: possible endorsement/partnership with Dan (S&C coach, NI/Ireland clients) — joint templates, commission; maybe joint pre-Nationals camp. Not confirmed.

## Hub structure
4–6 topic pages (not 100 tiny pages, not one giant PDF). Each page: hero, sticky clickable contents list, short scannable sections, "related" links, subtle coaching link at the end.
1. **New to ILCA** — boat age vs sail numbers, what a good price is, sail/entry costs, towing/roof-racking/strapping, kit, where events are listed, parents' guide (where to stay/eat in Weymouth).
2. **Moving up classes** — Opti/Topper → ILCA 4 → 6 → 7, weight ranges, timing.
3. **Season & qualification** — season structure, National Opens, ranking ladders, Youth Worlds/Euros pathway (data in bts-calendar/events-2026-27.md).
4. **Campaign management** — planning a season, budgeting, training choices, international events.
5. **Fitness, nutrition & psychology** — links to partner S&C.
6. **Pathway** — how sailors progress to squads/Olympic route.

## Venue guides
Sailing guide + parents' guide per venue. Weymouth/Portland first (USP: tide, swell refraction, local knowledge nobody else publishes). Then Poole, Hayling, Lymington, Warsash, Vilamoura. Can link to BTS Race Navigator tidal models.
- Template built: `beta/venues/weymouth/` (see bts-beta-site). Content model from James & Kai (11 Nov 2026): **ILCA-specific and technical**, written by ILCA sailors. Per wind direction AND per area (harbour vs bay behave differently in the same direction): where you race, which side pays as a percentage ("top left about 70:30", never a guarantee), technique by leg and tack (body position in inches, mainsheet vs steering, which gybe in a cross swell), "watch for". Annotated map (wind bends, where you overstand). Historic wind = Windfinder stats, credited, not passed off as ours.
- Parents' half: event-day timeline, stay/eat/watch, a travel cost calculator (fuel at ~30 mpg towing, nights, entry, ferry). International venues add ferries, charter, flights and journey time.
- Ask the British team what they'd let us read/publish from their Weymouth notes (not confirmed).
- Later: season templates per sailor type with a map route + costs, and a "travel information" directory (STATUS.md has the list).

James/Kai record a voice note per topic → send transcript → clean it (keep their voice), draft section copy into the page template, mark facts to verify. Old ILCA UK new-to-class guide exists (dated) — use for facts only, rewrite.
Tone: British English, direct, technically grounded, friendly to parents. No serial comma.
