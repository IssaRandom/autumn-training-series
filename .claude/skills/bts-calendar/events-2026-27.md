## UK youth qualification (policy Oct 2023 v4 — confirm current with youth@ilca.uk)
Ladders: ILCA 4 boys U18, ILCA 4 girls U18, ILCA 6 boys U19, ILCA 6 girls U21, ILCA 7 U21. Last 7 qualifier events within 18 months, best 4 count (best 3 if <5 events). Missed event = finishers+1. Ranking date = event's initial application close. Places allocated in ladder order to applicants. RYA (not ILCA UK) selects World Sailing Youth Worlds + British Youth squads. Blog #186: youth ladder unchanged until 2027 Nationals → expect change after.
Inference (not stated): ILCA 4 Youth Worlds places rest mainly on 2026 results; spring 2027 NOs can still count for ILCA 6 Youth Worlds.
