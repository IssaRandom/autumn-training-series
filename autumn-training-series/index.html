The programme

Perform.

A structured autumn development programme for ambitious ILCA 4 and ILCA 6 sailors, connecting technical foundations with race-ready execution.

Across seven camps, the focus develops from core technique and boat handling through pressure skills, starting, tactical positioning and race strategy. The final blocks turn event learning into a practical winter training plan.

01

Current squad experience

Coaching delivered by current ILCA 7 British Sailing Squad sailors.

02

Technical detail

Focused work on speed, handling, starting, racecraft and decision-making.

03

Personalised feedback

Video analysis and individual post-camp coaching summaries.

04

Structured development

A connected programme rather than isolated training weekends.

Your coaches

The training environment

Designed for high-performing sailors, open to all.

The programme is aimed primarily at sailors competing nationally who have international aspirations. Groups will be organised by experience and capability to create productive training for every sailor.

01

High-intensity water time

Purposeful sessions that create enough repetition to make technical changes stick.

02

Technical drills

Speed, manoeuvres, mark roundings and boat-handling work with a clear objective.

03

Racecraft development

Starting, positioning, strategy and tactical decision-making developed in context.

04

Video and feedback

Onshore analysis and a personalised post-camp summary to guide the next training block.
