The training environment

Designed for high-performing sailors, open to all.

The programme is aimed primarily at sailors competing nationally who have international aspirations. Groups will be organised by experience and capability to create productive training for every sailor.

01

High-intensity water time

Purposeful sessions that create enough repetition to make technical changes stick.

02

Technical drills

Speed, manoeuvres, mark roundings and boat-handling work with a clear objective.

03

Racecraft development

Starting, positioning, strategy and tactical decision-making developed in context.

04

Video and feedback

Onshore analysis and a personalised post-camp summary to guide the next training block.

Practical information

Frequently asked questions

Everything supplied about the series is set out here. Items awaiting a policy decision are clearly marked.

Who is the training intended for?

Ambitious young sailors, primarily those competing nationally who have international aspirations. Newly transitioned sailors and experienced international competitors are both welcome.

Which ILCA classes can attend?

ILCA 4 and ILCA 6 sailors.

Do sailors need to attend the full series?

No requirement to attend the full series.

How will sailors be grouped?

We aim to have one ILCA 4 group and one ILCA 6 group.

What is included in the camp price?

On-water coaching, onshore video analysis and a personalised post-camp coaching summary are described as part of the camp.

When do bookings close?

Bookings normally close three weeks before each camp. A £20 late booking fee may apply after that deadline.

How do I book?

Use the Google Form linked from every camp. You can select the training opportunities that interest you.

Will sailors receive individual feedback?

Yes. The programme includes detailed onshore video analysis and personalised post-camp coaching summaries.

Where will joining instructions be sent?

Final joining information will be sent separately.
