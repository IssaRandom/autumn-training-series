Cancellation policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

We know plans change. This policy explains what happens if you need to cancel, or if we have to.

If you cancel

Tell us as soon as you can by replying to your booking email or messaging us on WhatsApp.

  • More than 28 days before the camp: full refund, or move to another camp.
  • Between 28 and 7 days before: 50% refund, or a full credit towards another camp this season.
  • Less than 7 days before, or not turning up: no refund, unless we can fill your place from the waiting list.
  • Illness or injury with a note: we will always try to offer a credit.

If you haven’t paid yet, cancelling costs nothing.

If we cancel

  • If we cancel a whole camp (for example too few sailors, coach illness or venue problems), you get a full refund or a move to another camp, your choice.
  • If a group can’t run, we tell you as early as we can, and no later than 7 days before the camp where possible.

Weather

Sailing depends on the weather. If conditions aren’t safe, we run shore-based sessions instead: video review, rigging, tactics and fitness. We don’t refund days changed for weather, but if a whole camp can’t go ahead we treat it as a cancellation by us.

Refunds

Refunds go back by bank transfer to the account you paid from, within 14 days.
